# Problem Statement

## Overview

Organizations struggle with manual complaint management systems that lack transparency, accountability, and efficiency.

## Current Problems

**For Users:**
- Cannot track complaint status
- No proof of submission
- Repeated follow-ups needed
- No visibility into resolution timeline

**For Admins:**
- Manual tracking is time-consuming
- Cannot prioritize effectively
- Complaints get lost or forgotten
- No performance metrics

**For Management:**
- No visibility into trends
- Cannot measure response times
- Lack of data for decisions

## Solution

A digital complaint management system that provides:

**User Portal:**
- Submit complaints with categories and priorities
- Track status in real-time
- View complaint history

**Admin Portal:**
- Centralized dashboard with metrics
- Update status and priority
- Search and filter complaints
- Generate reports
